**Document ID:** `SWHNK-HNK-VOCABULARY-3000-GDD-PDD-V1`

## 1. Product goal
Build a governed HNK lexical core of **3,000 lexical units** capable of supporting an HNK course with communicative coverage designed toward an operational B2 scope. The number 3,000 is an HNK product target, not an official CEFR word-count requirement.

The target is split cumulatively as:

| Level | Cumulative | New at level |
|---|---:|---:|
| A1 | 500 | 500 |
| A2 | 1,000 | 500 |
| B1 | 1,800 | 800 |
| B2 | 3,000 | 1,200 |

## 2. Core principle
`3,000 lexical units != 3,000 arbitrary strings`.

A counted unit must represent one governed lexical/conceptual head. Transparent inflection, automatic derivation, spelling variants, duplicated senses, phrases and constructions do not automatically create additional lexical-count credit.

Two metrics must therefore coexist:

- **HNK3000 CORE LEXICAL COUNT** — governed lexical units counted once.
- **HNK SURFACE VOCABULARY** — all licensed surface forms generated by lexicalization, derivation, composition and grammar.

The surface vocabulary may become much larger than 3,000 without inflating the core count.

## 4. HNK-3000 counting contract
A lexical item becomes **HNK3000_COUNT_ELIGIBLE** only when all required fields are governed:

1. stable lexical-unit ID;
2. semantic concept/definition;
3. exactly one primary domain;
4. zero or more secondary semantic tags;
5. target level `A1|A2|B1|B2`;
6. lexical/functional class;
7. provenance class;
8. lexical authority/lifecycle state;
9. G-ID form when a form has been authored/recovered;
10. IPA derived from authoritative glyphs;
11. safe transliteration only when governed;
12. morphology/family relation if applicable;
13. productivity state;
14. exact and near-form collision audit;
15. at least one approved usage or explicit `USAGE_PENDING` boundary;
16. human governance decision that permits inclusion in HNK-3000.

### Do not count automatically
- transparent inflections of the same lexeme;
- predictable productive derivatives unless separately lexicalized with distinct governed semantics;
- alternate spellings/transliterations;
- phrases and constructions;
- proper-name bridges such as external names;
- system/reference names unless explicitly admitted to the lexical core;
- glossless/unresolved forms;
- dependency-blocked forms;
- duplicate concepts represented by accidental aliases;
- generated forms that have not passed a human gate.

## 5. Semantic-slot-first pipeline
Every new HNK word should move through this order:

`DOMAIN SLOT -> CONCEPT DEFINITION -> DUPLICATE/COVERAGE CHECK -> PROVENANCE DECISION -> MORPHOLOGY STRATEGY -> G-ID FORM DESIGN -> PHONOLOGY/COLLISION QA -> CANDIDATE -> USAGE TEST -> HUMAN GATE -> REGISTRY -> CURRICULUM BINDING`

This enforces `SEMANTICS_BEFORE_FORM` and `GID_FIRST`.

No batch generator may jump directly from a Portuguese/English word list to canonical HNK forms.

## 6. 30 × 100 architecture
HNK-3000 is divided into **30 lexical domains of exactly 100 counted units**. Each lexical unit has one primary domain so the same word can never count twice. Secondary tags allow cross-domain search without changing the count.

The level allocation is globally fixed at:
- A1: 500
- A2: 500
- B1: 800
- B2: 1,200

The exact domain allocation is defined in `HNK_VOCABULARY_3000_DOMAIN_MATRIX_V1.json`.

## 7. Domain families
The 30 domains are grouped into three planning bands:

- **CORE (15 domains):** daily-life and immediate communicative survival, pattern `20 A1 / 20 A2 / 25 B1 / 35 B2`.
- **EXPANSION (9 domains):** broader concrete/experiential world, pattern `15 / 15 / 30 / 40`.
- **ADVANCED (6 domains):** abstract/cultural/technical reasoning. Five use `11 / 11 / 26 / 52`; the final logic/connectors domain uses `10 / 10 / 25 / 55`.

These distributions are curriculum allocations only. They do not determine HNK forms or meanings.

## 8. Existing inventory policy
The current project contains **53 registered lexical forms** across recovered and authored registries. They are the **seed inventory**, not automatically `53/3000` completed units.

Before counting them toward HNK-3000, a dedicated seed-eligibility audit must assign:
- primary domain;
- level;
- count eligibility;
- authority/lifecycle compatibility;
- unresolved/dependency-blocked exclusions;
- alias/derivation relationships.

This prevents `GATE`, glossless, BRIDGE, REFERENCE or dependency-blocked items from inflating progress.

## 10. Quality gates
A lexical batch cannot advance if any of these fail:
- duplicate concept or duplicate form ambiguity not reconciled;
- unsafe transliteration guessed;
- non-authoritative G-ID assignment;
- unsupported morphology presented as recovery;
- reference language selecting a form;
- numerology selecting a form;
- gloss inferred from family resemblance only;
- derived form counted as an independent word without lexicalization evidence;
- curriculum slot used as proof of language authority.

## 11. Dashboard metrics
The HNK-3000 dashboard should expose at minimum:

- recovered vs authored provenance;
- FROZEN/WATCH/CANDIDATE/GATE etc.;
- concepts awaiting form;
- forms awaiting semantics;
- collision failures;
- dependency-blocked items;
- productive families;
- surface-vocabulary estimate, separately from core count.

## 12. Development sequence
### V0 — Architecture
Approve 3,000 target, level targets, 30 domains and counting contract.

### V1 — Seed audit
Classify the existing 53 forms against HNK-3000 without changing their meanings or authority.

### V2 — A1 semantic blueprint
Create the first 500 **concept slots**, still without mass-generating HNK forms.

### V3 — Controlled lexical authoring factory
Author small human-gated batches using semantic-first, G-ID-first and collision-safe rules.

### V4 — Corpus and pedagogy
Bind approved lexical units to lessons, drills, OPI, stories and examples. Repetition adds pedagogical coverage, not independent linguistic evidence.

### V5 — A2/B1/B2 expansion
Repeat the same governed pipeline until 3,000 counted units are reached.

## 13. Non-goals of V1
This document does **not**:
- create any new HNK word;
- promote any existing lexical authority;
- assign meanings to unresolved historical forms;
- change grammar;
- change HNK40;
- approve B16 or any Sprint-B language gate;
- change L03 curriculum/runtime state.

## 14. Success definition
`HNK-3000 COMPLETE` requires 3,000 unique count-eligible lexical units, the 30 domain quotas filled, A1/A2/B1/B2 cumulative quotas met, machine-readable provenance/authority data, and enough governed grammar/constructions to use that vocabulary communicatively. Word count alone is never treated as proof of B2 competence.

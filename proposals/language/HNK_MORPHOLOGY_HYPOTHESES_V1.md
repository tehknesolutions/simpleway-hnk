# HNK Morphology Hypotheses V1 — Recovery Analysis

**Analysis ID:** `SWHNK-HNK-MORPHOLOGY-HYPOTHESES-V1`  
**State:** `RECOVERY_HYPOTHESES / NOT_CANON`  
**Corpus:** current recovered Master Lexicon + L01/L02 evidence

## 1. Rule of interpretation

Recurring strings are treated as distributional evidence, not automatically as morphemes. A hypothesis may guide source recovery and candidate evaluation, but it cannot create a lexical meaning or grammatical rule by itself.

## 2. Hypothesis H1 — `VAN` as location/place formative

**Confidence:** `INFERRED_STRONG`  
**Promotion:** source or human linguistic review required

Known forms:

- `VALI` = work / to work;
- `VALIVAN` = office;
- `TAYOVAN` = city;
- `PAROVAN` = library;
- `VANI` = recovered WATCH form, exact gloss lost;
- `VANUVALI` = recovered observed/watch form, exact gloss lost;
- `VANUVALAKALU` = recovered failure/test form, exact gloss lost.

The pair `VALI → VALIVAN` is especially informative: the semantic transition from work/action to office strongly supports a location/place reading for `VAN` in at least one historical formation.

This does **not** yet prove that every occurrence of `VAN` is a productive suffix.

## 3. Hypothesis H2 — final `-I` correlates with action/verbal readings

**Confidence:** `INFERRED_STRONG`  
**Promotion:** morphological review required

Recovered forms with known meaning that end in `I` include:

- `VALI` — work / to work;
- `PARAZAMI` — study / to study;
- `PARI` — see;
- `DAYI` — want / intention;
- `SALI` — need;
- `SAROSARI` — travel / to travel.

Every one of these known-meaning examples contains an action/verbal reading. `VANI` and `VANUVALI` also end in `I`, but their exact glosses are unrecovered and therefore cannot be used as semantic confirmation.

Provisional interpretation: final `I` may be compatible with, or historically associated with, action/verbal formation. It is not yet approved as a productive suffix.

## 4. Hypothesis H3 — `VANI` as a possible locative verb

**Confidence:** `AMBIGUOUS_BUT_HIGH_VALUE`  
**Target requirement:** `LIVE_RESIDE`

Evidence:

1. `VANI` is an actual recovered WATCH form, not a newly invented string.
2. Its exact gloss is unrecovered.
3. It appears in L02 experimental/watch instrumentation, proving it was intentionally tested.
4. If H1 (`VAN` ~ place/location) and H2 (`-I` ~ action/verbal) are both later confirmed, `VANI` would be structurally compatible with a locative verb such as be/live/reside.

Critical restriction:

> Do not assign `VANI = morar/residir` from morphology alone.

Current action: prioritize historical source recovery for `VANI` before authoring a new `LIVE_RESIDE` candidate.

## 5. Hypothesis H4 — `PAR-` family may encode learning/cognition domain

**Confidence:** `INFERRED`  

Relevant forms:

- `PARAZAMO` — school / study domain;
- `PAROVAN` — library;
- `PARAZAMI` — study / to study;

The family clusters around study, library and perception. This is suggestive of a knowledge/learning/perception semantic domain, but the internal segmentation is not source-confirmed.

Do not derive new `PAR-` words yet.

**Confidence:** `AMBIGUOUS`

## 7. Hypothesis H6 — `SAR-` family may encode temporal/trajectory domain

**Confidence:** `AMBIGUOUS`

Relevant forms:

- `SARADAYA` — origin / birthplace;
- `SARASALA` — time / duration;
- `SAROSARI` — travel / to travel;
- `SARU` — every day / habituality;
- `SAROSAL` — recovered confusion form with unknown gloss.

Origin, duration, travel and habituality share a broad spacetime/trajectory association, but this remains interpretive and must not be used as a derivational rule yet.

## 8. Implications for L01

### `WORK_WORKING`
Prefer reuse/binding review of `VALI`, already FROZEN, rather than new word creation.

### `LIVE_RESIDE`
Search for the historical gloss of `VANI` first. Only if recovery fails should a new candidate be authored.

### `AGE`
`SARASALA` provides time/duration support, but no recovered rule licenses deriving an AGE lexeme from it. Age may also be grammatical/constructional rather than a separate noun. Keep open.

### `WEEKEND`
`VAMUSARO` (rest/leisure period) and `SARASALA` (time/duration) are semantically relevant, but no approved compounding rule currently licenses a composed weekend term. Keep open.

## 9. Promotion requirements

A hypothesis may be promoted to an authored grammar/morphology rule only after:

- explicit corpus examples are listed;
- counterexamples are audited;
- segmentation is declared;
- semantic function is narrowly defined;
- productivity is tested against existing forms;
- collision/confusion risk is checked;
- human approval is recorded;
- canonical implementation occurs in the owning HNK language registry, not silently in the course repo.
